    AIRCRAFT IN VIEW

      WHAT AM I LOOKING AT
      Aircraft
      Colour is altitude — the ramp in LAYERS, low to high. The line ahead is where it will be in a minute; the dots behind are where it has been.
      Overhead
      Amber: within the overhead ring and low enough to hear. The chip at the bottom names it, or counts down to the next one whose track passes over home.
      Red
      Military, or an emergency squawk (7500 hijack, 7600 radio failure, 7700 emergency) — those flash. A red CA link joins two aircraft within 3 NM and 1,000 ft of each other.
      Blue stripes
      Police and other law-enforcement aircraft.
      Data block
      Callsign · altitude and climb ↑ / descent ↓ · speed and type · operator · route. VFR means squawk 1200: not talking to a controller.
      Routes
      From published airline schedules, and only shown when the aircraft is actually near that route — a reused flight number shows nothing rather than something wrong.
      Styles
      LAYERS → STYLE, or tap a swatch to preview it for ten seconds. ☾ ☀ ◐ picks dark, light or auto (follows the sun at home).
      Keys
      T theme · S style · L layers · F find · H home · ? this help · Esc close
